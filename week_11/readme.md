# Customer Segment Profiles

This document describes the characteristics and behavioral patterns of each customer segment identified through clustering analysis.

---

## Segment 1: Premium Spenders

### Profile Overview
- High average spending
- Long-term customers
- Low churn tendency
- High engagement with services

### Key Characteristics
- Higher monthly and total charges
- Prefer long-term contracts
- Less price sensitive

### Business Interpretation
Premium Spenders contribute the highest revenue and show strong loyalty. Retaining this segment is critical for business stability and profitability.

---

## Segment 2: Budget Conscious

### Profile Overview
- Price-sensitive customers
- Moderate engagement
- Cost-driven purchasing decisions

### Key Characteristics
- Lower monthly charges
- Prefer short-term or flexible contracts
- Higher response to discounts

### Business Interpretation
This segment focuses on affordability. Targeted discounts and value bundles can improve retention without hurting margins.

---

## Segment 3: Young Professionals

### Profile Overview
- Digitally active
- Moderate-to-high churn risk
- Trend-oriented behavior

### Key Characteristics
- Medium spending capacity
- High usage of digital payment methods
- Shorter customer tenure

### Business Interpretation
Young Professionals require frequent engagement through digital channels and personalized offers to reduce churn and increase loyalty.

---

## Conclusion
Each segment demonstrates distinct behaviors and value propositions. Segment-specific strategies enable optimized marketing, improved retention, and higher overall business impact.
