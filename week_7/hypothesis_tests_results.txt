STATISTICAL HYPOTHESIS TEST RESULTS

1. T-Test (East vs West Sales)
Result: Significant difference in regional sales (p < 0.05)

2. One-Way ANOVA (Product vs Sales)
Result: No strong product-based sales difference (p > 0.05)

3. T-Test (Churn vs Monthly Charges)
Result: Churned customers have higher monthly charges (p < 0.05)
